## HRMIS – Human Resource Management Information System

HRMIS is a modern Human Resource Management Information System built with Laravel, PHP, Filament, and React. It is designed to help small organizations efficiently manage their employees, HR operations, and administrative tasks through a centralized platform.

The system includes separate login portals and role-based access for HR administrators, moderators, and employees, ensuring that each user can access the features relevant to their responsibilities.

**Key Features:**
- **Role-Based Access Control:** Separate access for HR, moderators, and employees.
- **Employee Management:** Manage employee profiles, personal information, job details, and employment records.
- **Attendance Management:** Track employee attendance, working hours, and leave requests.
- **Leave Management:** Submit, review, approve, and manage employee leave applications.
- **Payroll Management:** Organize salary records and payroll-related information.
- **Department Management:** Manage departments, positions, and organizational structure.
- **Recruitment Management:** Organize job openings, applications, and recruitment processes.
- **Performance Management:** Monitor employee performance and maintain evaluation records.
- **Reports and Analytics:** Generate reports to support HR operations and decision-making.
- **Centralized Dashboard:** Access important employee and HR information in one place.

HRMIS provides a practical and user-friendly solution for streamlining HR processes, improving administrative efficiency, and organizing workforce information within small organizations.
